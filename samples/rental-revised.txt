RESIDENTIAL RENTAL AGREEMENT

1. Parties and purpose
Asha Example (Landlord) lets Flat 4, Sample House, Pune to Rohan Sample (Tenant) for residential use. These are fictional parties and premises.

2. Term
The tenancy starts on 1 October 2026 and ends on 31 August 2027. Renewal requires a written agreement signed by both parties.

3. Rent and deposit
The Tenant must pay INR 23,000 per month by the fifth day of each month. A refundable security deposit of INR 69,000 is payable before possession. The Landlord may deduct unpaid rent and documented damage beyond ordinary wear. The remaining deposit must be returned within 30 calendar days after possession is returned.

4. Late payment
If monthly rent remains unpaid after the fifth day, a one-time late fee of INR 500 applies for that month. No daily late fee is specified.

5. Early termination and notice
Either party may terminate this agreement with 90 calendar days of written notice. If the Tenant leaves before 31 March 2027, the Tenant must also pay an early termination charge equal to one month's rent. The method of delivery and when notice is considered received are not specified.

6. Repairs and access
The Tenant is responsible for damage caused by misuse. The Landlord is responsible for structural repairs. The Landlord must give 24 hours' notice before entry, except in an emergency.

7. Deposit schedule
The security deposit balance will be returned within 30 calendar days after possession is returned, subject to documented deductions.

8. Governing law and disputes
This agreement states that Indian law applies and disputes are subject to courts in Pune, Maharashtra. It does not describe a mediation process.
