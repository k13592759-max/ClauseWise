FREELANCER SERVICES AGREEMENT

1. Parties and services
Mira Example (Freelancer) will create a five-page website for Sample Studio (Client). All names are synthetic.

2. Fees
The total fee is INR 80,000. The Client must pay INR 40,000 on signing and INR 40,000 within 15 calendar days of delivery. No late fee is specified.

3. Delivery and changes
Delivery is due on 30 November 2026, provided the Client supplies content by 1 November 2026. Two rounds of revisions are included. Additional work requires a written price agreement.

4. Intellectual property
Ownership of the final deliverables transfers to the Client only after full payment. The Freelancer retains ownership of pre-existing tools.

5. Termination
Either party may terminate with 14 calendar days of written notice. The Client must pay for completed work. The agreement does not define how partially completed work is valued.

6. Confidentiality
Both parties must keep non-public business information confidential for two years after termination, except information already public or disclosure required by law.

7. Governing law
This agreement states that Indian law applies. The forum for disputes is not specified.
